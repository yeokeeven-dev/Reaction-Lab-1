Why this ratio: acetic acid (CH₃COOH) reacts with sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO₃) roughly 1:1 by mole. 5% vinegar has about 0.00833 mol acetic acid per ml, so we scale baking soda mass to match — with a slight excess baking soda assumed absorbed, since it's cheap and prevents leftover acid smell.
